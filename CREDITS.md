# Credits

The hub's code is its author's. What it is drawn and heard with:

- **Fonts**: Inter, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif and Archivo, each under the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1. The licence and each family's copyright notice are in `licenses/OFL-*.txt`.
- **Icons**: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), ISC licence; some are derived from Feather (MIT).
  Both texts are in `licenses/Lucide-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Interface sounds**: [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds),
  CC0: `licenses/kenney-CC0.txt`.
- **Textures and the wordmark**: generated by the project's own tools, the wordmark from the
  fonts above.
- **Flight sounds**: the landing and the wind were synthesized for this project. The sonic boom
  and the take-off are Roblox library sounds, played by id.
- **Flight animations**: Roblox marketplace animations, played by id. They belong to their makers.
- **Flight effects** (`BasedHub/fx/bh_flight_pack.lua`): particle settings rebuilt from captures
  of two Roblox flight games, drawing Roblox textures by id. Those effects and textures belong to
  their makers.
